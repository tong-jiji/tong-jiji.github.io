About Me

I am a graduate student at Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics (NUAA). My research focuses on real-world image super-resolution, diffusion models, image restoration, and computer vision.

Research

My current work explores reliable diffusion-based restoration for real-world low-resolution images, with particular interest in improving structural fidelity and suppressing visually plausible but incorrect details.
